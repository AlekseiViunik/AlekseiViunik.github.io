Delete your account data

Effective and last updated: 24 September 2026

Cargo Mess is developed by the independent developer Aleksei Viunik (the "Developer"). This page explains how to request deletion of the data associated with your Play Games account, separately from the game's general Privacy Policy.

How to request deletion

  1. Open Cargo Mess, go to Settings, and note the Privacy support code shown there (it starts with "ID:"). Cargo Mess never collects your email address or Google account name, so this code is the only way the Developer can find your records.
  2. Send an email to alexviunik@gmail.com with the subject line "Cargo Mess data deletion request" and include the Privacy support code from step 1.
  3. The Developer will locate and delete the consent audit records (see below) matching that code within 30 days and confirm by email once complete.

What is deleted and what is retained

  • Consent audit records (your recorded advertising consent choices, stored in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore and identified by your Privacy support code) are deleted within 30 days of a verified request.
  • Local game data (progress, settings, currency, diagnostic log) lives only on your device and is not part of this request - it is removed automatically when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.
  • Purchase and transaction records handled by Google Play (such as the ad-removal purchase) are controlled by Google and may be retained by Google for periods required by law, independent of this request; see Google's own privacy controls for those records.
  • Play Games account data itself (your Google/Play Games profile) is controlled by Google, not the Developer; use Google's own account tools to manage or delete it.

For general privacy questions not related to account deletion, see the Privacy Policy.
