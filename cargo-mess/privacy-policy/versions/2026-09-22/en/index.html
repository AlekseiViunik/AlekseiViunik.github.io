Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 22 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the independent developer of the Android game Cargo Mess (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the game and this website.

1. Information stored locally

Cargo Mess stores game progress, current game sessions, earned in-game currency, settings, locally created levels, and a rolling diagnostic log on your device. This information is used to run the game, restore progress, remember your choices, and troubleshoot errors. It is not sent to the Developer automatically. You can normally remove it by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app.

2. Google Play Games

If you sign in through Google Play Games, the game accesses your Play Games player identifier and display name to authenticate you and display your player name. Cargo Mess does not request access to your Google Account email address through this sign-in. Google processes information under its own Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising and consent choices

Cargo Mess uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to show banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements, and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and record privacy choices where required.

Depending on your region, choices, device settings, and the kind of ad available, Google and advertising partners may process:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID where available;
  • app launches, ad views, taps, video views, and other interactions;
  • device, app, and diagnostic information used for ad delivery, measurement, analytics, security, and fraud prevention.

Where the law requires it, advertising-related processing and storage occur only after the required consent flow. You can revisit available privacy choices from the game’s settings. You can also reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings. A purchase that disables ordinary advertisements stops the game from requesting those ads, but transaction records and previously collected information remain subject to the applicable retention rules.

To document privacy choices, the game sends a separate consent audit record to Google Firebase Cloud Firestore in a European region when UMP supplies a consent signal. The record contains random installation and event identifiers, the IAB TCF consent string or a separate IAB GPP signal when available, the consent decision status, app version, platform, device language, identifiers of the published message and policy versions, the Google CMP identifier, and the time the record reaches Firestore. If the device is offline, the record waits in an app-private queue until delivery; the receipt time can therefore be later than the moment you made your choice. The record does not contain your name, email address, Android advertising ID, precise location, or a device fingerprint.

This record documents a choice, including a refusal or later withdrawal; it does not decide whether the game may request ads. UMP remains responsible for applying your current choice. The game uses anonymous Firebase Authentication and Play Integrity App Check when submitting records. Client access is limited to creating a record; clients cannot read, change, or delete existing records.

For more information, see How Google uses information for advertising and Google Business Data Responsibility.

4. Purchases

Optional purchases, such as disabling advertisements, are processed by Google Play. The Developer does not receive your full payment-card or bank-account details. Google may provide the game and the Developer with product, purchase-status, order, and transaction identifiers needed to deliver, restore, support, and account for the purchase. Google handles payments under its own terms and privacy policy.

5. Diagnostic reports and support

The game keeps a rolling diagnostic log locally. If you choose the in-game command to send a report, Cargo Mess prepares an email in an email app on your device. Nothing is sent until you review and send that email yourself.

A report may contain the app and Unity versions, device model, operating system, screen size, timestamps, current game mode and level, gameplay events, error messages, and stack traces. The email provider also processes your email address and message under its own policy. We use a submitted report only to answer your request, diagnose faults, prevent abuse, and improve the game.

6. Information you export

Levels or other files you create remain on your device unless you explicitly export or share them. The destination service you choose then handles those files under its own privacy terms.

7. Legal bases

Where the GDPR or similar law applies, information is processed as necessary to provide the game and requested features, comply with legal obligations, pursue legitimate interests such as security, troubleshooting, and documenting privacy choices, or on the basis of consent where consent is required. The consent audit record supports accountability for the choice made, even when you decline advertising consent. You may withdraw consent through the available privacy controls without affecting processing that occurred before withdrawal.

8. Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by Google for AdMob, UMP, Firebase Authentication, Firebase App Check, Cloud Firestore, Play Games, Google Play distribution, and Google Play Billing; by the email service you select when contacting support; and by GitHub, which hosts this website. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users and the service, or in connection with a lawful transfer of the game. We do not sell information you send directly to us.

9. Retention and security

Local game information remains until it is overwritten, cleared, or the app is removed. The diagnostic history is a rolling file limited by the app, and each newly prepared report replaces the previous temporary report file. Support correspondence is kept only as long as reasonably needed to resolve the request, maintain security records, and meet legal obligations. Purchase and accounting records may be retained for periods required by law. Third-party services set their own retention periods.

Consent audit records in Firestore are kept while needed to document privacy choices, handle related requests, and meet legal obligations. The Developer reviews and deletes records when those purposes no longer apply, using the Firestore server receipt time to assess their age.

Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards are used, but no transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. International processing

Google, GitHub, and email providers may process information in countries outside your own. Where required, they use recognised safeguards for international transfers as described in their privacy documentation.

11. Children

If local law requires a parent or guardian to authorise a child’s use of the game or processing of information, the game should be used only with that authorisation. Please contact us if you believe a child provided personal information directly to the Developer without the required permission.

12. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or receive a copy of personal information, to object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority. We may need enough information to verify and fulfil a request. For information controlled by Google or another provider, use that provider’s privacy controls as well.

13. This website

This static website contains no analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, contact forms, or cookies set by the Developer. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, so GitHub may process ordinary web-request information such as IP addresses and browser details under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

14. Changes

We may update this policy when the game, service providers, or legal requirements change. The latest version and its effective date will remain available at this URL.
