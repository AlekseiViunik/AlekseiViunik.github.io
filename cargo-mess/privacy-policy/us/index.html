US State Privacy Notice

Effective and last updated: 4 October 2026

This notice supplements the Privacy Policy of the Android game Cargo Mess, developed by the independent developer Aleksei Viunik (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”). It applies to residents of California and of other US states whose privacy laws give consumers the rights described below, such as Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas, Oregon, and others (together, “US state privacy laws”). It is available in English only.

1. Information we handle

The list below gives the categories of personal information that may be collected through the game, using the terms of US state privacy laws. We do not ask for your name, email address, postal address, or payment-card details, and the game does not use accounts of its own.

  • Identifiers — the Android advertising ID and app set ID where available, IP address, a Play Games player identifier and display name if you sign in, and random installation and event identifiers used for the consent audit record.
  • Internet and device activity — app launches, advertisement views, taps and video views, device model, operating system and app version, and diagnostic information.
  • Approximate location — a coarse location derived from your IP address. We do not collect precise geolocation.
  • Commercial information — purchase status of optional purchases, such as disabling advertisements, provided by Google Play. We never receive your full payment-card or bank-account details.
  • Privacy-choice records — the consent or opt-out signal supplied by Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP), the decision status, and the time of the record.

We do not knowingly collect or use sensitive personal information, as defined by US state privacy laws, to infer characteristics about you.

2. Sources and purposes

This information comes from your device when you use the game, from Google services the game uses (Google Mobile Ads / AdMob, UMP, Firebase, Play Games, Google Play), and from you when you contact us. It is used to run the game and restore progress, show and measure advertisements, record and honor your privacy choices, process optional purchases, troubleshoot errors, answer support requests, and prevent fraud and abuse.

3. Disclosure, “sale” and “sharing”, and targeted advertising

We do not sell personal information in exchange for money, and we do not sell information you send directly to us.

Cargo Mess shows advertisements through Google AdMob. To deliver and measure advertisements, Google and its advertising partners may receive identifiers and activity information listed in section 1 (the advertising ID, IP address, and ad interactions). Under some US state privacy laws, this can count as a “sale” or “sharing” of personal information, or as processing for “targeted advertising” or “cross-context behavioral advertising”. You can opt out as described in section 5.

Other disclosures go to service providers acting on our behalf (Google Firebase for the consent audit record, Google Play for purchases, GitHub for hosting this website, and the email provider you use to contact us), or where law requires. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16 years of age.

4. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • know and access the personal information we hold about you and the categories of sources, purposes, and recipients;
  • delete personal information we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate personal information;
  • opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, of targeted advertising, and of profiling in furtherance of decisions with legal or similarly significant effects (we do not make such decisions);
  • limit the use of sensitive personal information (we do not use it for purposes that would trigger this right);
  • appeal our decision on a request, where the law provides for appeals; and
  • not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

5. How to exercise your choices

  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising. Where the game offers US state privacy choices through Google’s User Messaging Platform, you can make or change your choice there, and reopen it from the game’s settings (Privacy options) when it is available. Your choice is recorded by UMP and applied to advertising requests. You can also reset or delete the advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings, and manage Google’s ad settings at adssettings.google.com. If you prefer not to see ordinary advertisements, the game offers an optional purchase that disables them.
  • Access, delete, correct, or appeal. Email alexviunik@gmail.com with the subject line “Cargo Mess US privacy request”, say which right you want to use, and include the Privacy support code shown in the game under Settings (it starts with “ID:”). We do not hold your name or email address, so this code is the only way we can find records that relate to you. Deletion of the consent audit records is also described on the data deletion page.
  • Authorized agents. You may use an authorized agent to submit a request. The agent should include written authorization from you and the Privacy support code.

We may need to confirm that a request comes from the person it concerns, for example by asking you to send the request from the device or to confirm the Privacy support code. We will confirm receipt and respond within 45 days of receiving a verifiable request, and we will tell you if we need more time as the law permits. Information that Google controls (for example your Play Games profile or purchase records) is handled through Google’s own privacy tools.

6. Retention

Retention periods are described in section 9 of the Privacy Policy. In short: local game information stays on your device until you clear it or uninstall the app; consent audit records are kept for up to 3 years from the Firestore receipt time; support correspondence and purchase records are kept only as long as needed or required by law.

7. Children

Cargo Mess is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when the game, service providers, or legal requirements change. The latest version and its effective date remain available at this URL.

9. Contact

For privacy requests or questions about this notice, contact the Developer at alexviunik@gmail.com.
